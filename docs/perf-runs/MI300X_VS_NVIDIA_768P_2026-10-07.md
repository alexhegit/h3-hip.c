# MI300X vs published NVIDIA numbers — Prompt 1, 768p, 5 s

2026-10-07. One Instinct MI300X (gfx942, DORobot). This is not a row in
the 832×480 / fox-15s tables in
[`MI300X_COMPARE_2026-10-07.md`](MI300X_COMPARE_2026-10-07.md).

NVIDIA's published 768p workload is **1344×768**, 24 fps, **124 frames**,
**5 s**, **50 steps**, seed **0**. The prompt is Prompt 1 from the
[H3-OnDevice page](https://nvlabs.github.io/Sana/Sol-Engine/H3-OnDevice/),
which prints a compacted sentence and points the benchmark at
[`demo_prompt.json`](https://github.com/NVlabs/Sana/blob/sol-engine/models/minimax_h3/demo_prompt.json)
on the `sol-engine` branch. That JSON's `target.duration_seconds` is 10.
The RTX 5090 and H100 configs override it to 5 seconds
(`H3_DURATION_SECONDS=5`, `width=1344`, `height=768`, `steps=50`,
`seed=0`). This run uses that override.

Sources for the NVIDIA cells:

- [H3-OnDevice](https://nvlabs.github.io/Sana/Sol-Engine/H3-OnDevice/) — RTX 5090 ladder, warm, batch 1.
- [models/minimax_h3](https://github.com/NVlabs/Sana/tree/sol-engine/models/minimax_h3) — H100 row, and the same 5090 baseline / full-opt pair.

## What was timed here

One Instinct MI300X, default **INT8** DiT, seed **0**. Process E2E is
`/usr/bin/time` until the process exits. `all-opt` is `--token-reduction`
on the 50-step dense job. FastH3 and VSA+TAEH3 replace `--steps` with
four evaluations at **999 / 749 / 500 / 250**, keep 50 layers, and require
`--reuse 1`. They are not stacked with token reduction, `--fbc`, or
`--sol-attn`.

Shared flags: `--profile -d /mnt/doscratch/MiniMax-H3 -p "$(cat prompt1.txt)" --width 1344 --height 768 --seconds 5 --layers 50 --reuse 1 --seed 0`. Dense and all-opt pass `--steps 50`. The 4-step rows pass `--steps 20`; the LoRA overrides that to four evaluations.

```sh
# dense
-o outputs/mi300x-prompt1-768p-5s-dense.mp4

# all-opt
--token-reduction -o outputs/mi300x-prompt1-768p-5s-tr.mp4

# FastH3
--fasth3-lora /mnt/doscratch/FastH3/dense-datafree/adapter_model.safetensors \
  -o outputs/mi300x-prompt1-768p-5s-fasth3.mp4

# VSA + TAEH3
--fasth3-lora /mnt/doscratch/FastH3/vsa-datafree/adapter_model.safetensors \
  --vsa --taeh3 /mnt/doscratch/FastH3/taeh3.safetensors \
  -o outputs/mi300x-prompt1-768p-5s-vsa-taeh3.mp4
```

`prompt1.txt` is the `prompt` field of `demo_prompt.json`. Each output
is **1344×768**, 24 fps, **124 frames**, duration **5.175 s**, H.264 + AAC.

## Published NVIDIA times, same 768p · 5 s workload

NVlabs say these are warm runs, and that the table is a same-hardware
speedup, not a cross-GPU latency ranking. Full opt is Sol-Attn plus a
cross-step cache (TeaCache on the 5090 config). Their transformer and
VAE dtypes in the 5090 config are **BF16**. The H100 cell is **4 GPUs**.

| device | GPUs | baseline | full opt | their speedup |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| RTX 5090 | 1 | 1045.4 s | **231.2 s** | 4.52× |
| H100 | 4 | 81.47 s | **22.89 s** | 3.56× |

RTX 5090 ladder on the H3-OnDevice page, same 1344×768 · 5 s · 50-step job:

| stage | time | vs their baseline |
|---|---:|---:|
| baseline, unmodified reference | 1045.4 s | 1.00× |
| kernel optimization | 971.6 s | 1.08× |
| + Sol-Attn | 708.3 s | 1.48× |
| + cross-step cache | 231.2 s | 4.52× |

The same README also lists GB200×8 (27.21 → 6.88 s), RTX 4090×1
(2239.22 → 504.33 s), and A100×4 (217.32 → 61.28 s) at 768p · 5 s, plus
DGX Spark at **480p**, not 768p. Those rows were not re-measured here.

## Head-to-head

Ratios use MI300X **process** time. A number in the MI300X columns is how
many times that MI300X path is faster than the published cell when the
cell is slower, and the other way around when the published cell is faster.

| published cell | time | dense 668.15 s | all-opt 439.64 s | FastH3 96.51 s | VSA+TAEH3 62.22 s |
|---|---:|---|---|---|---|
| RTX 5090 baseline | 1045.4 s | MI300X 1.56× | MI300X 2.38× | MI300X 10.8× | MI300X 16.8× |
| RTX 5090 + Sol-Attn | 708.3 s | MI300X 1.06× | MI300X 1.61× | MI300X 7.34× | MI300X 11.4× |
| RTX 5090 full opt | 231.2 s | 5090 2.89× | 5090 1.90× | MI300X 2.40× | MI300X 3.72× |
| H100×4 baseline | 81.47 s | 4×H100 8.2× | 4×H100 5.40× | 4×H100 1.18× | MI300X 1.31× |
| H100×4 full opt | 22.89 s | 4×H100 29× | 4×H100 19.2× | 4×H100 4.22× | 4×H100 2.72× |

Denoise plus decode (658.4 / 429.8 / 84.6 / 46.1 s) is the closer match
to a warm NVIDIA number. Against RTX 5090 full opt at 231.2 s those four
are 5090 **2.85×** / **1.86×**, then MI300X **2.73×** / **5.02×**.

The paths are not the same recipe:

- NVIDIA full opt is **Sol-Attn + cross-step cache** on the stock 50-step
  BF16 model. `--fbc` and `--sol-attn` were not run on this 768p fixture.
- MI300X all-opt is **50-step token reduction**, still INT8.
- FastH3 is a **4-step LoRA**. VSA+TAEH3 adds sparse video attention and
  the tiny decoder. The picture is a different composition from dense,
  and TAEH3 is softer.
- The H100 row is **four GPUs**. The 5090 row and every MI300X row are one GPU.
