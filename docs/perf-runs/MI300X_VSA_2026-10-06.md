## Same-fixture comparison

Process E2E is `/usr/bin/time` until `h3: wrote`.

| path | E2E | denoise | sdpa / linear | video decode | attention |
|---|---:|---:|---|---:|---:|
| FastH3, 4-step INT8 | **30.12 s** | 10.878 s | 6.029 / 3.911 s | 9.628 s | 200 |
| VSA, 32-lane + BF16 gate | **118.70 s** | 85.390 s | 42.443 / 42.030 s | 9.759 s | 200 |
| VSA, MFMA tile + INT8 gate | **31.53 s** | 9.568 s | 4.384 / 4.226 s | 9.713 s | 200 |
| VSA, prefetch MFMA + INT8 gate | **32.61 s** | 9.044 s | 3.811 / 4.300 s | 9.723 s | 200 |
| FastH3 + TAEH3 | **27.72 s** | 10.910 s | 6.032 / 3.943 s | 3.443 s | 200 |
| VSA prefetch + TAEH3 | **26.02 s** | 9.152 s | 3.947 / 4.276 s | 3.488 s | 200 |

The 118.70 s row is the coalesced 32-lane fine kernel plus a BF16
`to_gate_compress` GEMM. It is kept so the later kernel is not compared
with the scalar 1665 s SDPA run. That scalar run (29 min 23 s) is not a
performance result.

The 31.53 s row quantizes the gate with the same INT8 weight path as
the rest of the DiT and evaluates selected 64-token tiles with the
rocWMMA QK/PV fragments used by dense CDNA flash. `H3_VSA_MFMA=0`
selects the 32-lane kernel. Phase walls: text encoder 2.538 s, DiT
load 7.833 s, Euler denoise **9.568 s**, audio VAE 0.512 s, video VAE
9.713 s. Denoise other is 0.895 s. Profile peak on the denoise line is
**23.897 GiB**. Log: `h3: FastH3 merged 856 adapter tensors`.

Denoise is faster than dense FastH3 (9.568 s vs 10.878 s) and SDPA is
faster (4.384 s vs 6.029 s). Linear stays next to the FastH3 INT8
figure (4.226 s vs 3.911 s). Process E2E stays next to 30.12 s because
video VAE does not move (9.713 s, tiles 4×2 at 272 px). Audio VAE stays
0.512 s.

## Prefetch kernel (2026-10-07)

The fine kernel now builds a compact list of selected tile ids and
issues the next K/V global load while the current 32-row chunk is in
the MMA, the same overlap dense CDNA flash uses. The gate GEMM reuses
the activation QKV already quantized. `H3_VSA_MFMA=0` still selects the
32-lane kernel. Tile lists longer than 2048 fall back to that kernel.

Two idle-GPU launches of this binary, same command and seed:

| launch | E2E | text | DiT load | denoise | sdpa / linear | video VAE |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---|---:|
| first | 33.20 s | 3.635 s | 9.171 s | **8.853 s** | 3.682 / 4.255 s | 9.725 s |
| warmer text cache | **32.61 s** | 2.438 s | 9.566 s | **9.044 s** | 3.811 / 4.300 s | 9.723 s |

The table above quotes the warmer launch. Denoise is 0.5–0.7 s ahead of
the 9.568 s MFMA row, and SDPA is 4.384 → 3.68–3.81 s. Linear stays
about 4.3 s. Process E2E does not beat 31.53 s or 30.12 s: DiT load on
these launches was 9.2–9.6 s against 7.833 s on the 31.53 s row, and
video VAE is still ~9.7 s. The two mp4s are byte-identical.
