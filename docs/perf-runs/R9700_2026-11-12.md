## What moved

- **Dense SDPA paths** (dense, TR, fbc, FastH3, Pika): −11% to −27%. The
  longer the sequence, the larger SDPA's share and the larger #6's gain:
  832×480 · 5 s −11% to −17%, 864×480 · 15 s −14% to −20%, 1344×768
  −17% to −27%.
- **Video VAE** (#5 on top of the v0.19.0 BF16 VAE work): 21.7 → 14.9 s at
  832×480 · 5 s, 62 → 41.5 s at 864×480 · 15 s, 55 → 36.7 s at 1344×768 · 5 s,
  163 → 109 s at 1344×768 · 15 s.
- **VSA + TAEH3 gains least** (−3% to −8%): VSA runs its own sparse
  attention kernel, which #6 does not change, and TAEH3 replaces the video
  VAE, so #5 does not apply. For the same reason each TAEH3 row gains a
  little less than the same path with the VAE.
- **Sol-Attn**: −13% to −21% up to 1344×768 · 5 s, −11% at 1344×768 · 15 s,
  where the long sequences take the sparse dynamic-route kernel.
- **fox-s2 / fox-fast** stay load-bound (~98 GiB of weights per process on a
  59 GiB host): 33.6 / 46.8 s against 35.9 / 46.1 s. With the disk cache
  (#4) a repeated fox-s2 prompt skips the text encoder: **12.9 s**.
- Against MI300X the video rows are now 1.9–3.8× (were 2.2–4.4×).
