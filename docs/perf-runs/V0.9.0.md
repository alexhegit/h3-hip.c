## What to compare against later

- **Fox s2 E2E:** 82.9–87.3 s band from two timed repeats. A new change has to
  beat this *and* keep md5 `1731f95c…`.
- **Fox s2 denoise GPU:** 6.29–6.50 s. If this moves, the kernels moved.
- **Fox s2 I/O phases:** text + DiT load are cache-noisy; do not declare a win
  from a single E2E sample. Use `tools/bench_*.sh` interleaved A/B.
- **Fox-fast denoise GPU:** 28.37 s / 11 evals. This is the number that tracks
  the published M5 Max 16.69 s denoise profile.

Apples-to-apples vs antirez/h3.c tutorial CLIs (four-step and token-reduction
included): [`VS_UPSTREAM.md`](VS_UPSTREAM.md).
