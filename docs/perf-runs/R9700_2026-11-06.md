# Radeon AI PRO R9700 (gfx1201) — MI300X matrix replay, 2026-11-06

One AMD Radeon AI PRO R9700 (RDNA4, gfx1201, 64 CUs, 29.86 GiB VRAM,
PCIe 5.0 x16). Host: 16 cores, 59 GiB RAM, `/tmp` is tmpfs. Branch `r9700`
on `9dc75b1` with the uncommitted gfx1201 port and the memory-placement and
wave32 SDPA changes. `make HIP_ARCH=gfx1201`, TheRock ROCm wheel (HIP 7.16).
`HIP_VISIBLE_DEVICES=0`. Default INT8 DiT. One case at a time.

Process E2E is `/usr/bin/time` until the process exits. Denoise, SDPA and
linear are the `--profile` `H3 DiT` lines. Decode is the video VAE wall, or
the `TAEH3 decoded … in` time. MI300X numbers are the published ledgers
([`MI300X_COMPARE_2026-10-07.md`](MI300X_COMPARE_2026-10-07.md),
[`MI300X_2026-09-18_full.md`](MI300X_2026-09-18_full.md),
[`MI300X_VS_NVIDIA_768P_2026-10-07.md`](MI300X_VS_NVIDIA_768P_2026-10-07.md),
[`MI300X_2026-09-21_1344x768-15s-tr-sol.md`](MI300X_2026-09-21_1344x768-15s-tr-sol.md),
[`../PIKA.md`](../PIKA.md), [`../SOL_ATTN.md`](../SOL_ATTN.md)), not a
same-day rerun.

## Reading the gap to MI300X

Dense video is 2.8–3.3× MI300X on every canvas. SDPA runs the gfx1201
prefetch / transposed-V WMMA kernel at about 77 TFLOP/s, roughly 80% of the
card's BF16 matrix peak. Grouped-scale INT8 (FC2, attention out) was
65 TOPS against 222 TOPS for the plain INT8 GEMM; row-chunked
strided-batched hipBLAS brings it to about 112 TOPS. The rest of that gap is
the 14 int32 group planes, which a bit-identical reduce has to write and
read back.

  because only `FL2VA/` is downloaded here.

  The killed fbc try logged `rel -nan`; the clean rerun and a 6-step
  repro with and without `H3_INT8_HOST=1` show finite values. The rows above
  are the clean reruns.
