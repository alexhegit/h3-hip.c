2026-10-09. Same machine and 32 GiB VRAM carveout as
[`HALO_ONDEVICE_2026-10-05.md`](HALO_ONDEVICE_2026-10-05.md).
Tree: `fasth3-halo` @ `4ae61a2` (same commit as `main`). Build:
`make HIP_ARCH=gfx1151`. Base weights `/home/amd/HF-MODELS/MiniMax-H3`.
Adapters live outside the tree at `/home/amd/HF-MODELS/FastH3-4-step-LoRA`
(`dense-datafree` 1.4 GiB, `vsa-datafree` 5.0 GiB, `taeh3.safetensors` 22 MiB).

This is not the 2026-09-12 `bench/` scoreboard. MI300X FastH3 at 30.12 s
is a different GPU; it is not a ratio for this machine.

Every new row is the 832×480 · 5 s on-device fixture: seed 42, INT8 DiT,
no token reduction, no Sol-Attn, no `--fbc`, no GPU sampler, no INT8 VAE.
`--fasth3-lora` replaces the step count with 4 evaluations at timesteps
999, 749, 500, and 250, and forces 50 layers and `--reuse 1`.

```bash
./h3 --profile --fasth3-lora /home/amd/HF-MODELS/FastH3-4-step-LoRA/dense-datafree/adapter_model.safetensors \
  -d /home/amd/HF-MODELS/MiniMax-H3 \
  -p 'Cinematic push-in on a starship bridge: a captain in a high-collared navy tunic stands silhouetted at the observation window as a dreadnought armada charges its hyperdrives. A blinding flash, the bridge shudders, and the fleet is gone.' \
  --width 832 --height 480 --seconds 5 \
  --layers 50 --reuse 1 --seed 42 \
  -o outputs/halo-fasth3-4step-int8.mp4
```

`--taeh3` adds
`/home/amd/HF-MODELS/FastH3-4-step-LoRA/taeh3.safetensors`.
`--vsa` switches the adapter to `vsa-datafree/adapter_model.safetensors`.
Default `H3_VSA_SPARSITY` is 0.9. gfx1151 does not take the CDNA MFMA fine
kernel; the launch stays on the scalar fine kernel.
