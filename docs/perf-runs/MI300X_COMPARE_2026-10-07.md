# MI300X (gfx942) — 5 s and 15 s side by side

2026-10-07. One machine (DORobot, Instinct MI300X VF), default INT8 DiT,
seed **42**. Process E2E is `/usr/bin/time` until `h3: wrote`. The two
canvases are different prompts, resolutions, step counts, and layer
counts. Do not read a 5 s cell against a 15 s cell as the same workload.

`all-opt` means `--token-reduction` on that canvas. It is not combined
with `--fbc`. The older 15 s bundle `H3_GPU_SAMPLER=1 H3_INT8_VAE=1
H3_TOKEN_REDUCTION=1` (~2.4 min, denoise 113 s, INT8 VAE 24.6 s) is not
a row in these tables.

FastH3 and VSA reject token reduction, `--fbc`, and `--reuse` other than
1, and they keep 50 layers. Their schedule replaces `--steps` with four
evaluations at timesteps **999, 749, 500, 250**.

## 5 s

Input: starship-bridge prompt, `--width 832 --height 480 --seconds 5
--seed 42`. Output: **832×480**, 24 fps, **124 frames**, **5.167 s**,
H.264 + AAC.

| path | what ran |
|---|---|
| dense | `--steps 50 --layers 50 --reuse 1`, full video VAE |
| all-opt | same, plus `--token-reduction` |
| `--fbc` | same 50 steps, `H3_FBC_REL=0.15`, head 2, tail 2; 9 full / 41 skip |
| FastH3 4-step | dense-datafree LoRA, 4 evaluations, 50 layers, reuse 1, full VAE |
| FastH3 + TAEH3 | that row plus `--taeh3` |
| VSA | vsa-datafree, `--vsa`, sparsity 0.9, tile 64, keep 29 of 280 video tiles, prefetch MFMA, full VAE |
| VSA + TAEH3 | that row plus `--taeh3` |
