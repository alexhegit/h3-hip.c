# MI300X (gfx942) — fox-15s canvas, FastH3 / VSA / TAEH3

2026-10-07. DORobot, AMD Instinct MI300X VF (`gfx942`). The GPU was idle
(`No KFD PIDs`) before the first launch. The three runs then shared one
process sequence on that GPU. Binary is the prefetch VSA build. Model
`/mnt/doscratch/MiniMax-H3`. Seed **42**.

## What was timed

`bench/fox-15s.sh` is 864×480, 362 frames, 15.083 s, 20 steps, 45 layers,
`--reuse 2`, `--token-reduction`, seed 42. FastH3 rejects `--reuse` other
than 1, layer counts other than 50, and token reduction. VSA rejects
token reduction as well. These runs keep the script's prompt, canvas, and
seed, and use the flags the 4-step path accepts:

```bash
./h3 --profile -d /mnt/doscratch/MiniMax-H3 \
  --fasth3-lora /mnt/doscratch/FastH3/dense-datafree/adapter_model.safetensors \
  -p "$(the fox-15s.sh prompt)" \
  --width 864 --height 480 --seconds 15 \
  --steps 20 --layers 50 --reuse 1 --seed 42 \
  -o outputs/mi300x-fox15s-fasth3.mp4
```

`--steps 20` is overridden. The log prints 4 evaluations at timesteps
999, 749, 500, 250. VSA swaps in
`vsa-datafree/adapter_model.safetensors` and `--vsa`. The third run adds
`--taeh3 /mnt/doscratch/FastH3/taeh3.safetensors`.

VSA geometry, printed once: `VSA tile 64 sparsity 0.90: 24 prefix tiles, 756 video tiles, keep 76`.
Latents for the TAEH3 line: **107 × 30 × 54**.

20 steps and 45 layers. `--fbc` is reuse 2 without token reduction.
`fox-15s.sh` is the token-reduction script. The historical "all
optimizations" cell in `docs/PERFORMANCE.md` is about **2.4 min** with
denoise **113 s**; the measured successor of that TR path on this machine
is the 2026-10-05 `fox-15s.sh` row.

1.91×. `fox-15s-fast.sh` at 117.9 s stays ahead of FastH3 with the
full VAE and behind FastH3 plus TAEH3 and both VSA rows.
